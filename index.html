Biomedical Statistics & Data Science

Statistical and AI methods
inspired by biological questions.

We develop general statistical, deep learning, and AI methods inspired by challenges in high-throughput biology. Sequencing, chemical genomics and flow cytometry motivate new ideas in computational statistics, statistical foundations and continuous optimization. Our research spans compositional data analysis, microbial chemical genomics, human and microbial single cells, microbiomes and oceanographic data.

Research

We develop computational statistical, deep learning, and AI methods for sequencing, chemical genomics and flow cytometry. Applications span microbial ecosystems, single cells and host–microbe interactions.

Join us for your thesis

How to apply

We welcome Bachelor’s and Master’s students in Statistics, Data Science, Bioinformatics and related programmes. Tell us about your interests and background so we can discuss a suitable thesis project. Topics, supervision and data access are agreed individually.

  1. Choose a direction

    Explore the topics below, then describe the thesis you want to do: your research question, planned approach and intended goals. You may propose an idea connected to our research.

  2. Introduce yourself

    Include your degree programme, university, relevant courses and programming or research experience. A CV and current transcript of records are required.

  3. Get in touch

    Email Christian L. Müller your thesis proposal, motivation and a clear timetable with start date, milestones and intended submission date. Attach your CV and current transcript of records.
