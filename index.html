Biomedical Statistics & Data Science

Statistical methods
inspired by biological questions.

We develop general statistical methods inspired by challenges in high-throughput biology. Sequencing, chemical genomics and flow cytometry motivate new ideas in computational statistics, statistical foundations and continuous optimization. Our research spans compositional data analysis, microbial chemical genomics, human and microbial single cells, microbiomes and oceanographic data.

Research by topic

Research

Our research develops general statistical methods, computational statistics and continuous optimization, inspired by sequencing, chemical genomics and flow cytometry. Applications span microbial ecosystems, single cells and host–microbe interactions.
